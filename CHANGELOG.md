# What's new

## 17 November 2026 (after v0.1.6)

### Tag files in chat with #
- Type **#** in a chat and the app suggests **files** from Documents
  (then job cards, equipment, tools, stock, projects and purchases). Pick
  one and it goes into your sentence as a blue tag: "Read **#LOTO
  procedure** before starting". Several per message.
- Tapping a tag opens it. A tagged file also shows a small card (PDF,
  code, version, size). Nothing is attached or copied: the tag points at
  the file in Documents, so it always opens the newest version.
- Forwarded messages keep their tags (migration 0053).

## 17 November 2026 (v0.1.6)

### Chat fixes from phone testing
- The green chat button no longer floats over Chat itself (chats, calls,
  meetings, a conversation) or over a call; it only checked the page
  underneath.
- **Meetings** has its own **Schedule a meeting** button: pick the group,
  then the time.
- Missed calls read like WhatsApp: "Missed voice call · 02:30" (in a group,
  "Missed voice call from Nyasha Ncube"). Finished calls use a green icon.
- The Calls list no longer puts a stray letter before the date
  ("S Sun, Nov 15").
- The red counter on the green chat button now adds missed calls to the
  chats with unread messages.
- **Light is the default** for new users (and anyone who never picked one);
  dark stays one tap away on the sun button or in Settings → Appearance.
  Chat keeps following the phone's light or dark setting.
- **Last seen** works reliably: the app now records it when it opens,
  comes back and every two minutes while open, not only when it closes
  (phones often paused the app before that got through, so chats showed
  "Direct chat"). The header reads "Online" or "Last seen today 02:50";
  the words "Direct chat" are gone.
- On phones, leaving the app ends **Online** straight away (the
  connection used to linger a few minutes in the background).

## 16 November 2026 (v0.1.5)

### ➕ New: only things you start
- New in the sheet: **New project** (who manages projects), **New
  purchase** (who manages purchases), **Schedule a meeting** (pick the
  group, then the time) and **New chat or group**.

  you are on it.

  in three columns. Every card is in the brand colours (burgundy, rose,
  blue); Lend a tool is no longer amber.

### Side bar on every page
- On a computer or tablet (wider than 720 points) the side bar now shows on
  every page, including Chats, Checklists, Inventory and the other Menu
  pages, not only on Home, Jobs and Menu.
- **Collapse** at the bottom shrinks it to icons; **Expand** brings the
  names back. The app remembers your choice. On narrower screens it starts
  collapsed.
- The bar is the menu on wide screens: ➕ New, Home, Jobs and Chat (with
  unread count), then every page from the Menu tab in groups — **Work**,
  **Stores**, **Planning**, **Me** — and Settings at the bottom. There is
  no separate "Menu" entry, so nothing is listed twice. Notifications and
  Documents show their counts.

  bar, where Menu still lists everything.

### Chat works like WhatsApp (Phase 1)
- **Ticks** on your messages: a clock while waiting for signal, one tick

### Calls show Calling, then Ringing (as on WhatsApp)
- A direct call shows **Calling…** until the other phone actually rings,
  then **Ringing…**; the timer starts only when they answer.
- The other phone reports that it is ringing as soon as the incoming call
  shows, even when the app is closed (migration 0043, now the `push-received` function).

### Minutes in the department's format
- Meeting minutes now follow the department's paper minutes: meeting type,
  venue, chairperson and minute-taker; an attendance table with positions;
  site updates; an executive summary; the numbered action table with who is
  responsible (as initials) and the due date or status; a project status
  table; the next meeting's proposed agenda; and prepared, reviewed and
  approved by.

  agenda; attendance comes from who joined the call, with positions kept
  from the chat's last minutes. **Preview PDF** shows the result before
  approving.
- Database backups now run nightly (passphrase set); captions and AI minutes
  are on with a free Groq key.

### Safer permissions (issue #35)

  was out of service, and a **Repeat faults** sign at 3 or more in 30 days.

### Reserved stock (issue #34)
- Stock on approved material requests not yet issued counts as reserved;
  an item shows how much is reserved and how much is free. A project's BOQ
  counts only stock not reserved for someone else.

### Search and sync (issue #36)
- A **search** button on every tab finds job cards, equipment, tools,
  documents, inventory, projects, material requests and purchases by code or
  name.

### Backups (issue #31)

  not just row counts.

## 8 November 2026, evening

### Work done without signal is never lost (issue #17)
- Before, a phone without signal for about two and a half minutes gave up
  on changes waiting to send. Now no signal never counts as a failure: the
  app waits a little longer each time and sends as soon as it can. Changes
  an older version gave up on come back by themselves.
- **Settings → Waiting to send** shows everything not yet on the server,
  with **Try again** and **Discard** for anything the server refused, and
  **Send now**.
- **App errors** (Settings → Admin): errors on people's phones are reported
  and grouped, so they can be fixed.
- Faster lookups: every link between tables now has an index.
- Releases now also build the bundle the Play Store needs; the privacy
  policy is published with the web app.

## 27 October 2026 (on `main`)

### Calls on Android phones
- **Ringing calls.** A direct call rings the other phone with a full-screen
  incoming call and ringtone (Answer / Decline), even when the app is closed
  or the phone is locked. It stops after 45 seconds and becomes a missed
  call. The server sends call pushes to Android as high-priority data
  messages (`push-send`, type `call`, migration 0025).
- **Calls keep going when minimised.** While in a call the phone shows a
  "Call in progress" notification (tap to return, or hang up from it), so
  the microphone and sound keep working with the app in the background.
- **Speaker button** in the call to switch between earpiece and loudspeaker
  (video calls start on the loudspeaker).
- Samsung phones: if calls do not ring with the app closed, set the app's
  battery use to **Unrestricted** (Settings → Apps → Bravura Electrical →
  Battery).

The old addresses (`/today`, `/board`, `/more`, `/store`) redirect, so old links
still work.

- **➕ New** (middle of the phone bar, top of the desktop menu): one sheet to
  report a fault, add to today's report, do a checklist, lend a tool, send a
  message, add a document or open work requests. It only shows what the
  person's role allows. `lib/widgets/create_sheet.dart`
- **Home** is a to-do list (my jobs, checklists due, documents to sign, tools to
  return; for supervisors urgent jobs with nobody and requests to decide), four
  site numbers that open the matching jobs, and shortcuts on phones.
  `lib/screens/home_screen.dart`
- **Jobs** has one filter row (All active, Mine, Needs someone, Urgent, Overdue,
  Waiting, Done) and groups active jobs as Needs someone / In hand / Waiting.
  `JobFilter` in `lib/jobs/job_rules.dart` changed to these values;
  `openJobs(context, ref, filter)` opens the tab on a filter.
  `lib/screens/jobs_screen.dart` replaces `board_screen.dart`.
- **Menu** is a tile grid (`lib/ui/tiles.dart`: `TileGrid`, `ShortcutTile`).
- Every pushed screen has `AppBackButton` (`lib/widgets/app_back_button.dart`):
  it goes back, or to Home when opened from a link or a notification.
- Job history speaks plainly ("changed the equipment and the lock-out rule") and
  hides the system's own reminder stamps.
