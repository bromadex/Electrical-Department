Privacy policy

Bravura Electrical Department app · last updated 8 November 2026

The Electrical Department app is a work tool for the electrical team of Bravura's sites. It is used only by people the department gives an account to. This page says what the app keeps about you, why, and who can see it.

What the app keeps

  • Your account: name, work email, phone number if you add it, your role and home site.
  • Your work: job cards, comments, work-log entries, photos and files you add, checklists, daily report updates, tool and stock movements, material requests, signatures on tool issue forms and documents you sign for.
  • Chat and calls: messages, photos, voice notes and files you send in the team chat. Calls are not recorded unless a meeting's recording is switched on, which everyone in the call is shown.
  • Your device: a notification token so alerts reach your phone, and the app's version and type of device when an error happens in the app (to fix it). The app does not read your contacts, location or other apps.
  • On the phone itself: a copy of the data you use, so the app works without signal, and changes waiting to be sent. Signing out removes it.

Why

Only to run the department's work: assigning and tracking jobs, keeping tools and stock accounted for, safety records, reports and communication within the team. Nothing is used for advertising and nothing is sold.

Who can see it

  • People in the department, according to their role (for example, supervisors see job cards and reports; the store sees tool and stock records). Every change is logged.
  • The services the app runs on, which store and process the data for the department and for no other purpose: Supabase (database, files and sign-in), Google Firebase (push notifications) and Vercel (the web version). Data is sent to them encrypted.

How long

Work records are kept as long as the department needs them for its records and safety obligations. Records are archived rather than deleted so the history stays complete. Backups are kept for 90 days.

Your choices

  • You can switch notifications off in the app's Settings or on your phone.
  • To see, correct or remove your personal details, or to close your account, ask the department's app administrator. Your work records stay with the department, without your personal details where the law allows.

Children

The app is for employees and contractors of the department only and is not meant for children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published here with a new date.
